## Frequently asked questions

**Are your pieces food safe?**

Yes — all functional pieces are glazed with food-safe, lead-free glazes and fired to stoneware temperatures.

**Can pieces go in the dishwasher?**

Yes, though hand washing will help preserve the finish over time.

**Do you offer pottery classes?**

I currently focus on making rather than teaching, but I occasionally run workshops — follow my Instagram for announcements.

**Do you work with interior designers?**

Yes — I enjoy collaborating with designers on custom pieces for residential and commercial projects.

*I aim to respond to all inquiries within 24–48 hours. If you don't hear back, please check your spam folder or reach out via Instagram.*
